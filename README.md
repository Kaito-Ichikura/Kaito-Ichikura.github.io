## About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of System Information Sciences, Graduate School of Information Sciences, Tohoku University, Japan. My research focuses on proof theory, with particular emphasis on non-classical logics and arithmetic.

---

## Research Interests

- Proof Theory
- Non-Classical Logics (Subminimal, Minimal, Intuitionistic, and Intermediate Predicate Logics)
- Arithmetic
- Constructivity Principles
